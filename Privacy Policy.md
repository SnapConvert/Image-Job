# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** November 2026

At **SnapConvert**, accessible from [snapconvert.net](https://snapconvert.net), we prioritize your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we handle your uploaded files, and the measures we take to keep your data secure when you use our free online file conversion service.

---

## 1. File Handling & Data Retention

Your privacy and document security are our highest priorities:

* **Temporary Processing:** Files uploaded to SnapConvert are used exclusively to perform the requested file conversion. 
* **Automatic Deletion:** All uploaded files and converted output files are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers shortly after processing (typically within 3 to 4 hours).
* **No File Inspection:** We do not preview, copy, share, index, or analyze the contents of your uploaded files.
* **Client-Side Conversions:** For conversions processed directly in your browser using WebAssembly technology, your files never leave your local device.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

Because SnapConvert does not require registration or user accounts, we collect minimal information:

* **Usage Data:** We may collect non-identifiable technical data automatically sent by your browser, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URL, and the date/time of your request.
* **Conversion Metrics:** We collect anonymous aggregate statistics (e.g., total number of files converted, popular file format pairings) to monitor server loads and improve service performance.
* **No Personal Data:** We do not collect names, email addresses, payment details, or any personally identifiable information (PII).

---

## 3. Cookies & Analytics

* **Essential Cookies:** We use minimal, essential session cookies required for the website to function properly (such as remembering temporary conversion preferences).
* **Analytics:** We may use lightweight, privacy-focused web analytics to understand overall website traffic. These analytics tools do not track you across other websites or store personal identifiers.
* **No Third-Party Ad Tracking:** We do not sell, rent, or share your browsing activity with third-party advertising networks or data brokers.

---

## 4. Security & Third-Party Services

* **Data Encryption:** All data transferred between your browser and our servers is encrypted using standard HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS) technology.
* **Infrastructure Security:** Our servers are configured with strict access controls to prevent unauthorized access, loss, or misuse of temporary file data.
* **Third-Party Links:** Our website or repository may contain links to external sites (such as GitHub, Discord, or YouTube). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external platforms.

---

## 5. Changes & Contact Information

* **Policy Updates:** We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our technology, legal requirements, or service features. The latest version will always be available on this page with an updated date.
* **Contact Us:** If you have any questions, concerns, or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our official website at [snapconvert.net](https://snapconvert.net) or via our GitHub repository.
